> [!NOTE]
> WhatsApp integration is currently under development. At this time, Tars officially supports **Discord** as its primary communication channel.

WhatsApp will serve as a mobile-first, native communication channel for Tars. It will allow you to control your infrastructure naturally from your phone without needing a separate app or relying on a desktop.

### Future Capabilities

Once implemented, the integration will use secure protocols to establish a connection to your WhatsApp account as a Linked Device.

### Planned Message Triggers

| Trigger            | Use Case         | Example                        |
| ------------------ | ---------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Direct Message** | Private Commands | `Tell me the server status`    |
| **Voice Notes**    | Voice Processing | Tars will transcribe & process |

### Support Roadmap

We are currently refining the multi-channel architecture to ensure the highest security and reliability before enabling WhatsApp support for all users.

If you have feedback on which mobile platforms you'd like to see Tars on next, please let us know!
